## What is an SLA template?

An SLA template defines an objective, verifiable performance contract for offchain cross-border payouts. Anchors commit stake to a specific template version, and sending clients route payments knowing the exact speed target and financial remedy.

Templates are immutable once activated. When parameters require tuning, governance proposes a new version behind a timelock delay.

## Template parameters

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | `u32` | Unique template identifier (e.g. `901`). |
| `version` | `u32` | Version number (starts at 1). |
| `metric` | `Metric` | Currently `TimeToCompletion`. |
| `start_event` | `EventKind` | Event marking the beginning of measurement (`SenderDebitConfirmed` or `OnChainSettled`). |
| `end_event` | `EventKind` | Event marking delivery (`ReceiverCreditConfirmed` or `RecipientAcknowledged`). |
| `target_ms` | `u64` | SLA delivery target in milliseconds. |
| `grace_ms` | `u64` | Grace buffer in milliseconds before breach penalties apply. |
| `max_hold_ms` | `u64` | Maximum allowable cumulative pause duration for carve-outs. |
| `claim_window_secs` | `u64` | Maximum window after payment initiation during which claims can be filed. |
| `evidence_mode` | `EvidenceMode` | Mode of evidence required (`DualSigned`, `ProviderSigned`, or `ObserverOnly`). |
| `scope` | `Scope` | Asset, corridor, direction, and amount bounds. |
| `carve_outs` | `Vec<CarveOut>` | Allowed pause justifications. |
| `penalty` | `PenaltyModel` | Formula for calculating penalty compensation. |

## Live testnet template: Template 901

Template 901 is active on Stellar testnet and backed by Commitment 1:

- **ID & Version:** `901` (Version 1)
- **Target Duration:** 60 minutes (`3,600,000 ms`)
- **Grace Period:** 15 minutes (`900,000 ms`)
- **Maximum Hold:** 24 hours (`86,400,000 ms`)
- **Claim Window:** 7 days (`604,800 s`)
- **Corridor Scope:**
  - Asset: Testnet USDC (`CB2CFWENJ2M5JXWBGUOKO75QVIFJ2EY5WOIOHR6FFYTXAKGS2S3XOIM4`)
  - Direction: `Outbound` (Onchain Stellar payment to local fiat account)
  - Amount Range: 10 USDC to 5,000 USDC
- **Penalty Model:**
  - Model: `PerBreachBps`
  - Basis Points: 500 bps (5% of payment volume)
  - Cap: 50 USDC (`500,000,000` stroops)
- **Approved Carve-outs:**
  - `ReceiverBankHoliday`: Automated hold during weekend or national bank holidays.
  - `ComplianceHoldDeclared`: Cryptographically attested AML/sanctions review.
  - `InvalidBeneficiary`: Immediate notification when downstream bank rejects account numbers.

## Proposing and activating templates

Templates cannot be altered arbitrarily:

1. **`propose_template`**: Governance or providers submit a full `SlaTemplate` struct. The template status is set to `Proposed`.
2. **Timelock delay**: The template must wait for the registry timelock duration (`timelock_secs`, default 7 days) before it can take effect.
3. **`activate_template`**: Once the timelock elapses, anyone can call `activate_template(id, version)` to transition the status to `Active`. Providers may now bind commitments to this template.
4. **`deprecate_template`**: When retiring a template version, governance calls `deprecate_template`. Existing commitments continue until exit, but no new commitments may bind to deprecated templates.
